3.1 adds GPT-6.2, brings OpenResponses outside the chat with Shortcuts, Siri and the share sheet, and adds OpenResponses Pro, a one-time purchase for those.


GPT-6.2

• GPT-6.2 Sol is the default for new presets, and GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.1 Terra are in every model menu.  Your existing presets keep the model they have.

• On GPT-6.2 your system prompt goes in as the first message of the chat instead of as top-level instructions, so the API can cache it and you aren't billed full price for it on every turn.

• Request Options only offers the service tiers the model actually has.  A saved tier the model doesn't have gets left out of the request instead of failing.

• Presets on models OpenAI is retiring move to the replacements on OpenAI's deprecations page, like gpt-5 and o3 to GPT-6.1 Sol.


SHORTCUTS, SIRI AND THE SHARE SHEET

• "Ask OpenResponses" sends a question with one of your presets and an optional image, PDF or text file, and gives you the answer as text without opening the app.  It works from Siri, Spotlight, the Action button and the Shortcuts app.

• "New Chat in OpenResponses" opens a new chat with your text and files already in it, unsent.

• OpenResponses is in the share sheet for text, links, up to 10 images and up to 10 files.  Add a question, tap Save, and the next time you open the app it's all in a new chat.  Nothing gets sent from the share sheet.


OPENRESPONSES PRO

• Shortcuts and Siri, the share sheet and the new 12-hour prompt cache are part of OpenResponses Pro, a one-time $4.99 purchase with no subscription.  Everything that was already in the app stays free, and so do the new models and fixes, and you keep using your own OpenAI key.

• It's in Settings, with Buy and Restore Purchases.


ALSO

• Request Options has a 12-hour prompt cache lifetime next to the usual 30 minutes.

• Web search's blocked domains go to the API as a real filter now instead of a sentence in the instructions.

• When OpenAI's misalignment monitoring stops a request, the chat tells you so and doesn't keep retrying it.

• Connectors stops listing a service you've already signed in to, and if you still have an old Notion integration token next to a signed-in Notion account, it offers to remove the token.

• Web search sources that embed other content on their page load now instead of showing "Navigation blocked".


Still no account, still your own key, and your chat history stays on your phone.
