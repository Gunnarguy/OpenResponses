3.0 shows you what the model is sending to a tool while it's still writing it.


TOOL STEPS

• When the model calls one of the app's own tools, like the browser, Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Notion, Local Python or your Custom tool, its step fills in the arguments as the model writes them.  Before, the step stayed empty until the whole call had come in.  Tap the step to see them.


DECISIONS

• There's a new Decisions screen in Settings, on the Advanced tab under Developer Operations, for OpenAI's Decisions API.  You write some text or add a photo, ask it yes or no, pick-one or score questions, and it gives you back how likely each answer is instead of a written reply.  It runs on GPT-6 Luna, and you only pay for the input, at $0.10 per million tokens.

• The API Workbench has a Create decision endpoint too, so you can send the raw request yourself.


CALENDAR AND REMINDERS

• Events and reminders get added when the model leaves the time zone off a time, which it does a lot.  In 2.9 those failed, twelve out of twelve on one photographed schedule.  Now a time with no zone is read in your phone's time zone.
